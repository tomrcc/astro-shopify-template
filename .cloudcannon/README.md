# Field & Thread website

The shop's products, prices, stock and checkout live in Shopify. Everything around them is edited here: pages, the stories on product pages, collection intros, navigation and the announcement bar.

## Pages

Most pages are built from sections. Open a page in the Visual Editor, click any text to change it, and use the section controls to add, move or remove sections.

The **Featured products** and **Collection grid** sections let you pick products and collections from Shopify. Their titles, photos and prices come from Shopify.

## Product stories

A product story adds a tagline and sections to a product's page. To add one, click **+ Add**, choose the product, and save. The story then appears on that product's page, where you can edit it in the Visual Editor.

The product's title, photos, price, options and description come from Shopify. Change those in Shopify admin.

## Collection intros

A collection intro adds a heading, intro text, an image and sections to a collection's page. Which products appear, and in what order, is set in Shopify admin.

## Site settings

- **site**: the store name, navigation and footer.
- **announcement**: the bar across the top of every page.
- **shop**: button labels and messages for the cart and product pages.

## Parts that come from Shopify

Some parts of the site come from Shopify. In the editor they're marked with a purple icon; hover over or click the icon to see where to change them.

Prices in the editor are from the last build. The live site checks them with Shopify as shoppers browse. Adding to the cart is off in the editor.
