---
title: Care guide
description: How to wax, tune and store your Ridgeline board.
---

Wipe your board dry after every session, and store it somewhere cool with the bindings loosened.

## Waxing and edges

Hot wax the base every few days on snow. Detune the tips for park, and sharpen the edges before an icy trip.

## Repairs

Core shot or a cracked edge? Send it back and we'll fix it for free in the first two years.
