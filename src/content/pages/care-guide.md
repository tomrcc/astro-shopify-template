---
title: Care guide
description: How to wash, dry and repair your Field & Thread clothes.
---

Wash cold, inside out, with similar colours. Skip the dryer when you can: line drying keeps cotton soft and stops it shrinking.

## Down and puffers

Use a gentle cycle with a down-safe detergent. Tumble dry low with a couple of clean tennis balls to restore the loft.

## Repairs

Popped a seam? Send it back and we'll fix it for free in the first two years.
