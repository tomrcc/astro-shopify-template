---
title: Shipping and returns
description: How we ship your order, and how to send something back.
---

Orders ship within two working days, packed in recycled cardboard with no plastic fill.

## Shipping

Shipping is free on orders over $60. Coffee is roasted to order, so it may ship a day after the rest of your order.

## Returns

Unused gear can come back within 30 days for a full refund. If a ceramic piece arrives chipped, send us a photo and we'll replace it.
