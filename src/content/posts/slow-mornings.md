Most of us make coffee on autopilot. Push a button, check a phone, gulp it down. Making it by hand asks for ten minutes of attention, and gives a lot back.

## A small ritual

Boil the kettle. Weigh the beans. Watch the bloom rise. None of it is hard, but each step asks you to slow down before the day speeds up.

## The right cup

A mug you love to hold makes the ritual feel like yours. Ours are thrown in small batches, so the one you get won't look quite like anyone else's.
