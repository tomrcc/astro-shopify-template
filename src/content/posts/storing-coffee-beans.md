Coffee is at its best between a few days and a few weeks after roasting. How you store it decides which end of that window you're drinking from.

## The rules

- **Keep it airtight.** Oxygen is what makes coffee taste flat.
- **Keep it dark.** Light fades the oils that carry flavour.
- **Keep it cool.** A cupboard away from the oven is ideal.
- **Keep it dry.** The fridge is too damp and full of smells.

## What about the freezer?

Freezing works for beans you won't open for a month or more. Freeze them in small, sealed portions, and don't put them back once they've thawed.
