Water pulls flavour out of coffee faster when the grounds are fine and slower when they're coarse. Matching the grind to your brewer is the quickest way to a better cup.

## A rough guide

- **Fine**, like flour: espresso and stovetop pots
- **Medium-fine**, like sea salt: pour-over
- **Medium**, like sand: drip machines
- **Coarse**, like breadcrumbs: French press and cold brew

## Whole bean or ground?

Ground coffee goes stale within days. Whole beans keep their flavour for weeks. If you can, grind just before you brew. If you can't, buy ground coffee in small bags and use it quickly.
