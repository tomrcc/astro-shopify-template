Pour-over looks fussy, but it comes down to three things: fresh coffee, the right grind and a slow, steady pour. Get those right and the rest follows.

## What you need

- A dripper and a filter
- A carafe or a mug to brew into
- 15 g of coffee, ground medium-fine, about like sea salt
- 250 ml of water just off the boil

## The recipe

1. Rinse the filter with hot water to warm the dripper and wash out any papery taste. Tip the water away.
2. Add the coffee and shake it level.
3. Pour just enough water to wet the grounds, then wait 30 seconds while the coffee releases gas. This is called the bloom.
4. Pour the rest in slow circles, keeping the water level steady, until you've added it all.
5. Let it drain. The whole brew should take about three minutes.

## If it doesn't taste right

Sour or thin? Grind finer next time. Bitter or harsh? Grind coarser. Change one thing at a time, and you'll find your recipe in a few mornings.
